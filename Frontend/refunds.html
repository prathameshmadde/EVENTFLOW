Bank-Grade Escrow Guarantee

How Your Event Funds Are Protected

Unlike informal vendor deposits where money is difficult to recover, EventFlow retains milestone funds in an authorized escrow account. If an event manager fails to deliver contractually agreed deliverables, your funds are refunded according to the mediation protocol.

> 30 Days Notice 90% Full Refund Excludes non-recoverable custom design fee.
15 - 30 Days Notice 60% Escrow Return Vendor raw material deposits deducted.
< 15 Days Notice Mediation Audit Assessed based on fabrication proof.
